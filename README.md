# Corlabs

**We build software that moves businesses forward.**

Corlabs is an Argentina-based software company focused on building **digital products, SaaS platforms, and business-critical software**.

We operate both as a **product company** and as a **technology partner**.

On one side, Corlabs is a hub where we design, build, launch, and operate our own products. On the other, we partner with companies that need more than software development: we work to understand their operations, identify opportunities, and turn complex business processes into reliable digital systems.

## What we do

### Build products

We design and develop our own digital products, with a strong focus on SaaS and scalable software businesses.

We aim to move from idea to working product quickly while maintaining an architecture that can evolve as the product grows.

### Build for businesses

We work with companies on software that is deeply connected to how their business operates.

Rather than focusing on conventional websites or isolated development tasks, we work around **business workflows, operations, integrations, automation, and product development**.

Our goal is to become a technology and product partner capable of understanding both the software and the business behind it.

## How we work

We believe good software starts with understanding the problem.

Our approach combines:

* **Product thinking** — understanding what should be built and why.
* **Software engineering** — creating maintainable and scalable systems.
* **Business understanding** — designing software around real operational needs.
* **Automation** — eliminating unnecessary manual work and connecting systems.
* **Infrastructure ownership** — understanding and operating the environments where our software runs.
* **Continuous iteration** — shipping, measuring, learning, and improving.

## Engineering at Corlabs

We maintain our own development infrastructure and tooling to give our team control over the environments where our products are built and tested.

Our internal platform is based around containerized workloads, private networking, centralized HTTPS routing, private DNS, secrets management, persistent storage, and reproducible development environments.

We favor infrastructure that is **simple, observable, reproducible, and under our control**.

## Our principles

**Solve the business problem, not just the technical task.**

Technology is useful when it improves how a business operates or enables something that was previously impossible.

**Build for change.**

Products evolve. Architecture should provide enough structure to grow without turning early development into unnecessary complexity.

**Automate what should not require human attention.**

Repetitive operational work is usually an opportunity for better tooling.

**Own the system.**

Understanding the complete path from code to infrastructure makes us better at designing reliable products.

**Ship. Learn. Improve.**

Working software creates information. We prefer iterative development over trying to predict everything upfront.
